# 6. Microstructure, Defects, and Inelasticity

> *Continuum plasticity is a negotiated truce: we pretend the material is smooth while secretly knowing that dislocations, voids, and grain boundaries do the real work.*

Linear elasticity (Part III–IV) describes the copper wire's first response. Permanent deformation requires **inelastic constitutive laws**—plasticity, viscoplasticity, damage—that track internal variables summarizing microstructure. This part connects those laws to the **defects** they approximate.

Source material: *Elasticity & Inelasticity*, *Defects & Disorders in Materials*, *Foundations of Solid Mechanics*, and *Nonlinear Finite Element Analysis* notes.
