## Functional analysis style

Part II structure follows classical functional analysis notes (metric spaces → normed spaces → Hilbert spaces → operators → functionals), in the spirit of:

- Gongqing Zhang, functional analysis lecture notes (as adapted in [PolyKen/Functional-Analysis-Notes](https://github.com/PolyKen/Functional-Analysis-Notes))
- H. Brezis, *Functional Analysis, Sobolev Spaces, and Partial Differential Equations*, Springer
- P. D. Lax, *Functional Analysis*, Wiley
