# 7. Line Defects and Strain Hardening

> *A dislocation is a topological defect in the crystal lattice—and a computational object you can track, collide, and statistics.*

**Dislocation dynamics** (DDD) simulates collections of dislocation lines under applied stress. It sits between continuum plasticity and atomistics: lines are discrete, but the elastic field is continuum (linear elasticity with singular self-fields regularized at the core).

This part summarizes theory and the author's research on **link statistics during strain hardening** in f.c.c. copper—118 large-scale ParaDiS simulations revealing double-exponential link length distributions on active slip systems.
