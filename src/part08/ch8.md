# 8. Atomistic Simulation

> *If you need to know whether a dislocation cross-slips, a crack emits a specific emission, or a grain boundary slides—sometimes only atoms have the answer.*

**Molecular dynamics** (MD) integrates Newton's equations for $N$ particles with positions $\mathbf{r}_i$ and forces $\mathbf{F}_i = -\nabla_{\mathbf{r}_i} V$ from an interatomic potential $V$. It is the workhorse of atomistic modeling when electronic rearrangement is localized and classical nuclei suffice.

Source material: *Atomistic Modeling* notes, DeePMD/graphene studies, and LAMMPS/GPUMD workflows referenced in the author's repositories.
