# 9. Electronic Structure

> *Every spring constant in a harmonic approximation, every cohesive energy in an EAM fit, every reaction barrier in ReaxFF traces to electrons—and DFT is how we compute them.*

**Density functional theory** (DFT) solves for the ground-state electron density $\rho(\mathbf{r})$ instead of the $3N$-electron wavefunction—making atomistic-scale electronic structure feasible. DFT closes the multiscale ladder: it explains **why** atoms bond the way empirical potentials assume.
